General Instructions for Login

Important guidelines for students logging into the portal:

  • Your Enrolment Number is your official University Registration / Enrolment ID provided on your admit card or admission slip.
  • For first-time users, click on New User Registration to set your account password.
  • Please ensure your registered mobile number and email address are active to receive OTP notifications.
  • Never share your password or OTP with anyone.
  • If you have forgotten your password, use the Request Password Reset option below the login card.

Public Notices & Announcements

  • Notice #1: Examination schedule and form submission for current academic semester has been released.
  • Notice #2: National Internship Portal registration is active. Eligible candidates are encouraged to apply.
  • Notice #3: In case of technical issues with fee submission, contact the technical helpdesk at support.samarth@kunainital.ac.in.
